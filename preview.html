Everything You Need

Built for
serious readers

Every feature is designed to reward knowledge and make reading feel as competitive and rewarding as your favorite game.

🤖

AI-Generated Quizzes

Claude AI creates unique, accurate questions for any book in our library. Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.

⚔️

Elo Ranking System

A true competitive ladder — each quiz shifts your Elo points, with inactivity decay to keep the rankings fresh.

🔥

Streak Rewards

Build daily reading streaks and earn bonus multipliers. Miss a day and lose the streak — stay consistent.

🌍

Google Books Integration

Search millions of titles instantly. PagePoints pulls cover art, metadata, and reading level from Google Books.

🎨

Rank Themes

Unlock Fantasy, Sci-Fi, and Romance themes. Reach Grand Sage IV to unlock the exclusive Grand Sage theme.

📊

Detailed Stats

Track points earned, accuracy, level progression, current rank, peak rank, and lifetime reading history.
