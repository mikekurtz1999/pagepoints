How It Works

From picking a book to climbing the ranks — four simple steps.

1
Search for a book
Type any title or author. PagePoints searches Google Books and pulls the cover, author, page count, and reading level automatically.
🌍 Google Books API Millions of titles
2
Choose a difficulty & start the quiz
Pick Easy, Medium, or Hard. Claude AI generates 10 unique questions about that specific book — plot, characters, themes, and facts.
🤖 Claude AI Easy / Medium / Hard 10 questions
3
Answer & earn points
Each correct answer earns points multiplied by difficulty and accuracy. Finish with a high score to maximize your Elo gain.
Difficulty multiplier Accuracy bonus Streak multiplier
4
Climb the leaderboard
Your Elo updates after every quiz. Advance through 4 subdivisions per tier — from Page Turner all the way to Grand Sage. Stay active or your rank decays.
